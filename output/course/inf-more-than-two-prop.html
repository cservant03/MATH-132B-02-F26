    Subsection Homogenity

    Formula 182 Testing Homogenity.

    Requirements
    1. All of the samples must be simple random samples.
      🔗
      🔗
    2. All of the samples are independent of each other.
      🔗
      🔗
    3. All expected values must be at least five.
      🔗
      🔗
    🔗
    Hypotheses
    \(H_0:\) The probability distribution is the same for all samples.
    🔗
    \(H_a:\) At least one sample is different.
    🔗
    🔗
    Contingency Table
    🔗
    Given Information (Observed Values)
    🔗
    Sample 1 Sample 2 Sample 3
    Category 1 \(O_{11}\) \(O_{12}\) \(O_{13}\) Row 1 Total
    Category 2 \(O_{21}\) \(O_{22}\) \(O_{23}\) Row 2 Total
    Column 1 Total Column 2 Total Column 3 Total Grand Total
    Need to Calculate (Expected Values)
    🔗
    Sample 1 Sample 2 Sample 3
    Category 1 \(E_{11}\) \(E_{12}\) \(E_{13}\) Row 1 Total
    Category 2 \(E_{21}\) \(E_{22}\) \(E_{23}\) Row 2 Total
    Column 1 Total Column 2 Total Column 3 Total Grand Total
    \begin{equation*} E_{ij} = \dfrac{\text{Row } i \text{ Total} \cdot \text{Column } j \text{ Total}} {\text{Grand Total}} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    Test Statistic
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \chi^2 = \sum_{i=1}^{r}\sum_{j=1}^{c} \dfrac{\left(O_{ij}-E_{ij}\right)^2}{E_{ij}} \qquad \text{df}=(r-1)\cdot(c-1) \end{equation*}
    🔗
    R Functions

      🔗
      🔗
    • For critical values use qnorm
